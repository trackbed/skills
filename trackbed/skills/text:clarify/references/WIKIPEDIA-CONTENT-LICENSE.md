`ai-writing-patterns.md` is adapted from [revision 1383697236 of Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing&oldid=1383697236) (2026-11-04). Its contributors are identified in the [page history through that revision](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing&action=history&dir=prev&oldid=1383697236).

The local reference reorganizes, edits, and supplements the source material; it is not an unchanged Wikipedia page. Its modifications include:

- It condenses the page's sections, together with entries from humanizer and local entries, into 36 numbered patterns. Each has a short problem statement and Before/After examples, and most list words to watch.
- Before texts come from the page's examples, from humanizer, or are local, and some are shortened or extended. Most After texts are local rewrites; some come from humanizer.
- It omits Wikipedia-specific signs, such as markup, citation, and article-review indicators, that do not apply outside Wikipedia.
- It retains patterns adapted from blader/humanizer under the MIT License, including false ranges and sycophantic tone, and adds local patterns, including procedural self-reports, re-explained context, signposting, self-describing text, and formulaic document structure.
- On 2026-11-09 it was re-synced from revision 1366684471 to revision 1383697236:
  - It groups four of the signs the page now calls historical (promotional language, knowledge-cutoff statements, em dash overuse, and elegant variation) as dated older-model signs.
  - It reframes the filler and hedging entries to keep the constructions the page finds more typical of human writers.
  - It adds the page's current signs for vague connection wording, emphasis on how sources identify a subject, leftover scaffolding and placeholders, edit-summary tells, and help-desk tone in replies.
  - It merges the page's sign about procedural edit summaries into the local procedural self-reports entry.
  - It re-scopes entries whose upstream scope changed, and it credits the false-ranges entry to humanizer because the page does not list it.
  - It removes facts from After examples that their Before texts do not state.
