Blockers

New accounts cannot sign in

Route /login

Signing in with an account created today shows "Something went wrong" and stays on the sign-in page, so a new customer cannot reach the product at all.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open /signup and create an account.
  2. Sign out, then open /login.
  3. Enter the new account's email and password and choose Sign in.

Expected and actual

Expected
The board opens.
Actual
An error banner appears and the page does not change.

Evidence

401 /api/session
{"error":"profile_missing"}

Suggested fix

Create the profile row when the account is created, in src/api/signup.ts:38.

Major

The task title overlaps the due date on narrow screens

Route /board, at 375 pixels wide

Long titles run under the due date, so neither can be read on a phone.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open /board at a width of 375 pixels.
  2. Look at any task whose title is longer than one line.

Suggested fix

Let the title wrap, and move the due date to its own line.
