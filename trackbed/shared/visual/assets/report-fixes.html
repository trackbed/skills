Model packet readiness in the stock ledger

loans/checkout.ts:24 and catalog/availability.ts:38

Checkout, the catalog, and returns each decide on their own whether a returned packet is available, and they disagree. Giving the stock ledger that decision removes all three checks; patching each one would keep the disagreement.
